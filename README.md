### Introduction
Prompt engineering helps improve the quality of responses generated by AI systems. Different prompt patterns guide AI models in unique ways to produce accurate, structured, and meaningful outputs. In this experiment, various prompt types are applied to an AI-based customer support chatbot system used in manufacturing industries. The prompts are tested to understand how different prompting methods influence clarity, relevance, creativity, and response quality.

### 1.Comparative Analysis Prompt
### Definition
Comparative analysis prompting compares two or more technologies, concepts, or systems by identifying their similarities, differences, strengths, and limitations.

### Prompt
“Compare rule-based chatbots and AI-powered chatbots used in manufacturing customer support systems based on scalability, accuracy, maintenance, customer satisfaction, and response capability.”

### Output
Rule-based chatbots operate using predefined commands and fixed decision trees. They provide quick responses for repetitive customer queries but cannot manage complex conversations effectively.
AI-powered chatbots use machine learning and Natural Language Processing (NLP) to understand user queries intelligently. They can learn from customer interactions, provide personalized responses, and support complex conversations more efficiently.
Rule-based systems are easier to maintain for small applications, while AI-powered chatbots are more scalable and suitable for advanced manufacturing support systems.

### Purpose
This prompt helps analyze the advantages and disadvantages of different chatbot technologies and supports better decision-making for manufacturing industries.

### 2.Experiential Perspective Prompt
### Definition
Experiential perspective prompting asks the AI to generate responses from the viewpoint of a specific person, profession, or role.

### Prompt
“Imagine you are a customer support supervisor in a manufacturing company. Describe your experience using AI chatbots for complaint handling, product inquiries, and order tracking.”

### Output
As a customer support supervisor, AI chatbots help manage customer interactions more efficiently. Customers receive instant support for order updates, product details, and complaint registration without long waiting times.
One common challenge is handling unclear or emotional customer messages. Sometimes the chatbot fails to understand customer intent correctly.
To improve performance, the chatbot system is regularly trained using updated customer conversation data. Human support agents are also included for handling complicated customer issues.

### Purpose
This prompt encourages practical understanding by connecting AI concepts with real workplace experiences and challenges.

### 3.Everyday Functioning Prompt
### Definition
Everyday functioning prompts explain how a technology or system is used in daily activities or real-world environments.

### Prompt
“Explain how AI-powered chatbots are used in everyday manufacturing business operations.”

### Output
AI-powered chatbots assist manufacturing companies by answering customer questions, tracking orders, providing technical support, and managing complaints. Customers can communicate with chatbots through websites and mobile applications at any time.
The chatbot quickly processes customer requests and delivers suitable responses using company databases. Difficult problems are transferred to human support teams when required. This improves customer satisfaction, reduces response time, and increases operational productivity.

### Purpose
This prompt demonstrates the practical implementation of AI systems in daily industrial operations and customer service activities.
